FAQs

Asked before, answered here

Do you only work in the South West?

No. The firms above are the ones we know best. The practice is UK-wide. The phone is a Bristol number. The registered office is London.

We are smaller than 50 people. Are we too small?

Usually. Start the conversation anyway if the risk is oversized for the headcount: a regulated book, an acquisition to integrate, an insurer who will not wait.

We are bigger than 200 people. Are we too big?

Usually, for the fractional seat. A firm inside a larger group can still be the right fit for a fixed sprint. We say honestly at diagnose whether the fit is real.

Do you work with our existing MSP?

Yes. You keep the MSP if it works. We sit above it for the decisions, the evidence and the bill.

We are private-equity backed. Does that change the work?

No. The services are the six on the services page. What changes is the pace: acquisitions bring deadlines, and the work is scoped to them.

Can you make the firm AI-ready?

Yes, that is the point of the framework: readiness gets an owner. The seat owns it, and the AI services put it to work: the AI Opportunity Audit, Copilot readiness, and one production IT agent.

FAQs

We already have an MSP. Why do we need you?

You have an MSP. You still need a Head of IT. The MSP runs the queue. Someone above it owns the renewal, the questionnaire and the AI decision, and answers for them at the board. That is the seat we fill.

Do you work with firms outside 50-200 people?

Under 50, when the risk is oversized for the headcount. Over 200, when a clearly part-time seat makes sense. We say honestly at diagnose whether the fit is real.

Can you help with client and panel security questionnaires?

Yes. That is the CE+ / ISO 27001 sprint. We implement, an accredited assessor certifies, and the pack is written so a panel, an insurer or an enterprise client can read it. CE+ from £2,950; ISO 27001 from £15,000.

What changes on 26 April 2026?

The scheme itself gets harder: MFA on every cloud service and critical patches inside 14 days. If CE or CE+ is on the plan, we scope the work against the version you will actually be assessed on and say plainly where a control needs work.

Where does AI start for a firm like ours?

With the audit. The AI Opportunity Audit reads the firm, names where AI pays and what must be true of the tenant first, and returns a scored shortlist the board can decide on. From £4,950, fixed.

Will you train the partners on Copilot?

We make the tenant ready first, then run a 30-day pilot with a small group of the right people. Firm-wide training is a later decision, taken on the pilot's evidence.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

No. We do not sell helpdesk. Your MSP does out-of-hours, if you bought it.

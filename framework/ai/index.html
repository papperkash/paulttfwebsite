Questions

Is Copilot safe to switch on?

It is safe when the tenant is. Oversharing, stale permissions and unlabelled data are what make Copilot risky in a regulated firm. Governance fixes Purview and labels first, buys Copilot licences only for the people who need them, then runs a 30-day pilot with that group.

Have you put AI agents into production yourselves?

Yes. Call 0117 456 5486. The voice that answers is our own AI call agent, in production, on our own line. We run our practice on what we sell.

Do we need an AI policy before anything else?

You need a written position the board can read: what the firm uses, what it does not, who owns the risk, and what you would tell the insurer. It is usually the first deliverable in this domain, and it is short.

No. One workflow at a time, with a clear owner and a clear finish line. We build where an automation earns its keep, we measure what it saves, and then we decide together whether there is a next one.

What does AI governance mean for a regulated firm?

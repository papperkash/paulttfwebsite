Who it is for

The bill that grew by project.

This is for the firm whose Azure bill is now large enough that the FD wants a reading they can trust. The estate grew by project. Reservations, idle kit, and the wrong SKU never found an owner.

If the annual bill can fund a hire, it is worth a conversation. If it cannot, we will say so. This offer only runs when the spend is large enough to matter.

It often sits next to the Head of IT retainer. It can also be a single sprint with a finish line.

The engagement

A sprint the FD can read.

We sit with finance and with the people who actually run the estate. The bill is treated as a finance document: what is production, what is idle, what auto-renewed, and what a reservation would change.

The work is a fixed piece. We do not take a cut of the savings. Cuts, if they come, follow the reading, with an owner, and without breaking the estate.

The named senior who has taken 250+ servers to Azure and taken 80% off a live cloud bill does this work. That is the record. Every estate is its own bill.

A bill with an owner.

  • The waste that can come off

  • An owner for the next quarter

Questions an FD asks

Before you book.

How large is large enough?

If the cloud bill can fund a hire, it can fund a sprint. We will tell you if the spend is too small to justify the work.

Will you cut production to make a number?

No. The first deliverable is a reading the FD can trust. Cuts follow that, with an owner.

Do you take a share of the savings?

No. This is a fixed piece of work.

Is this only Azure?

This offer is Azure FinOps. The record includes a live estate moved to Azure. Other clouds can wait.

Can this sit alongside the Head of IT retainer?

Yes. The sprint has a finish line. The retainer is the seat, one or two days a week.

What does the FD receive on the last day?

A paper they can read, the actions that are safe to take, and a name for who owns the bill next.

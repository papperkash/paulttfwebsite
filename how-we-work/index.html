04

FAQs

Asked before, answered here

Do we have to keep the seat after the 90 days?

No. At day 90 you choose: keep the seat at one or two days a week, or take the gains in-house. Nothing obliges you to continue, and the place is stronger either way.

How long does each piece of work take?

Most of the work is built around 90 days of visible progress. The AI Opportunity Audit runs shorter, two to three weeks, because its job is to tell you where AI pays before you spend.

Who do you talk to first?

The people who actually run the estate: the MSP lead, finance, operations, whoever holds the passwords. Then the FD or MD who started the conversation.

Will you present to the board?

Yes. Every piece of work ends in something a board can read. That is what the 90 days is for.

Do you work on site?

When the estate needs it. Much of the work happens in the tenant, the bill and the evidence. The phone number is a Bristol number. The registered office is in London.

Is this IT support?

No. You keep the MSP if the MSP works. We sit above the helpdesk, on the decisions: the bill, the risk, the AI question, the board answer.

Can two services run in the same 90 days?

Sometimes. The seat pairs naturally with any of the sprints, and the audit often comes first because it tells you which sprint pays. We will not pile every sprint on a firm that needs one.

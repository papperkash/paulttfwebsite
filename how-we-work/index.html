The shape

Three steps. The same on every engagement.

  1. 01

    We sit with the people who actually run the estate.

    The MSP lead, the FD, the person who still holds the admin passwords. What to keep. What has been auto-renewing.

  2. 02

    A 90-day sprint with a board-readable outcome.

    Assurance, Copilot, FinOps, or a single production agent. One named finish line.

  3. 03

    One or two days a week after that, or we leave the place stronger.

    The retainer is the Head of IT seat. If the seat is filled, we stop.

Diagnose

A short, honest look.

The diagnose is time with the people who run the estate, the vendors, and the risk. We read the last cyber form, the last board question, and the contracts that keep signing themselves.

You leave that first piece knowing what to keep, what to stop, and which of the five offers is the ninety days. If none of them fit, we say so.

Ninety days

One finish line the board can read.

The sprint is one named piece of work. Cyber Essentials, CE+ or ISO 27001. Copilot readiness and a 30-day pilot. Azure FinOps when the bill is large enough. One production IT agent with one KPI.

The Head of IT retainer can run in parallel when the seat is empty. The sprint still has its own finish line. In ninety days the board has an operating model, the insurer has evidence, or the tenant and the bill have an owner.

The retainer

Fill the seat, or we leave.

After the sprint, one or two days a week on the leadership team if the seat still needs filling. You keep the MSP. We stay above it.

If the firm now has an owner, we leave the place stronger. The conversation stays on the seat.

Questions an FD asks

How this actually runs.

What do you need from us in the first week?

Access to the people who run the estate, the last cyber form, and the contracts that are about to renew. The MSP can stay in the room.

Can we buy only the sprint?

Yes. The ninety days have a finish line. The retainer is a separate decision.

Can we buy only the retainer?

Yes, when the seat is empty and the work is already clear. Most firms still start with a diagnose.

Do you work only in the South West?

The practice is used to South West wealth, IFA and SIPP firms, and we work nationally. The registered office is in London. The phone is a Bristol number.

How soon can you start?

After a conversation. We will not take a sprint we cannot finish.

Who does the work?

A named senior. Twenty years as Head of IT. The board meets that person.

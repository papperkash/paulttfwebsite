Who it is for

The empty seat.

This is for the firm that already has an MSP and still has nobody on the leadership team who owns IT. The FD became the department. The managing partner still gets the cyber form.

It fits 50-200 people in professional services, South West wealth, IFA and SIPP practices, and PE-backed roll-ups. A full-time IT director costs £130k before they have started. You need the seat filled without that hire.

The MSP keeps the tickets. The board still needs an owner for the answers.

The engagement

In the room, above the MSP.

We sit with the people who actually run the estate. The MSP lead, the office manager who still resets passwords, the FD who signs the contracts. We learn what to keep and what has been auto-renewing without an owner.

Then the work is the operating model, the vendors, and the board paper. One or two days a week. You keep your MSP if it works. We govern it.

After ninety days you either keep the retainer because the seat still needs filling, or we leave the place stronger. That is the same shape as the rest of the practice.

What you leave with

The board can name who owns IT.

  • A named Head of IT

    The person the board calls. One senior, in the room, one or two days a week.

  • Vendor governance

    The MSP and the other contracts sit under one owner. Renewals stop signing themselves.

  • An operating model

    Who does what, what the MSP owns, and what comes back to the leadership team.

  • Board paper

    A reading the FD and the managing partner can take into the next meeting.

Questions an FD asks

Before you book.

Do we have to change our MSP?

No. You keep it if it works. We sit above it and govern it. If the MSP is the problem, that shows up in the first weeks and we say so.

What do you actually do on those days?

Vendor reviews, the operating model, the board paper, and the decisions that have been sitting with the FD. The MSP still runs the tickets.

Who do you report to?

The FD or the managing partner. The seat is on the leadership team.

Can we start with one day a week?

Yes. One or two days is the offer. We agree the shape before we start.

Will the board meet you, or a bench?

A named senior does the work. The board meets that person.

What if we already have a good IT manager?

Then we sit above that person and the MSP, and we take the paper the board has been missing.

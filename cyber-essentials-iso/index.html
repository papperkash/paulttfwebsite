The form nobody can answer.

Questions an FD asks

Before you book.

We have a renewal in six weeks. Is that enough?

Sometimes. We will tell you in the first conversation whether ninety days, or a shorter CE pack, fits the date. We will not promise a certificate we cannot reach.

What is the difference between Cyber Essentials and CE+?

Cyber Essentials is the baseline most insurers now expect. CE+ adds a verified assessment. ISO 27001 is the management system buyers in enterprise, PE and the public sector ask for.

Do you write the policies and leave?

We implement the controls. The pack is evidence of that work.

Who issues the certificate?

An approved body. We prepare the estate and the evidence. We do not print our own badge.

Will this get us through client due diligence?

It gives you a pack and an owner. Large clients still ask their own questions. You will have answers.

You mention PCI DSS on the proof page. Is that this offer?

PCI DSS was implemented in a live estate as part of the named senior's record. This offer is Cyber Essentials, CE+ and ISO 27001.
